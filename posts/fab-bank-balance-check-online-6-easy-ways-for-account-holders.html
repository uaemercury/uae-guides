FAB Bank Balance Check Online: 6 Easy Ways for Account Holders

With a First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB) account, checking your balance takes seconds — on your phone, on a computer, at an ATM, or by calling the bank. FAB was formed in April 2017 when First Gulf Bank and National Bank of Abu Dhabi (NBAD) merged; NBAD no longer exists as a separate bank. FAB is licensed and regulated by the Central Bank of the UAE, and its official website is bankfab.com — type it yourself, and never follow a balance-check link sent by SMS or email.

The 6 ways at a glance

FAB Mobile app — fastest for most people. FAB Online Banking — best on a computer, with full statements. FAB ATM — best when you are out. Phone banking — best with no internet access. SMS banking — text BAL plus the last four digits of your account number to 2121. Statements and mini-statements — best for an older balance or tracking spending.

One warning first: if you hold a prepaid salary card such as a Ratibi card instead of a bank account, the app and online banking may not show your balance. A Ratibi card has no bank account behind it. Use the prepaid card portal instead.

1. FAB Mobile app

The FAB Mobile app is the quickest way for most account holders. It is on the App Store, Google Play and Huawei AppGallery. After login, your account and card balances appear on the home screen. The app also manages accounts and cards, shows transactions with logos, categories and smart filters, and sends real-time alerts. You can sign in with fingerprint or face. Open the app, log in, and look at your account on the home screen; tap it for recent transactions.

2. Online banking

FAB Personal Online Banking, at bankfab.com, is the best option on a computer. Log in and open the Accounts view to see your balances, recent transactions and history. You can also view and download statements there — useful for checking an older balance, or confirming when money came in or went out. If you cannot sign in, do not keep guessing your password. FAB locks online banking after three unsuccessful login attempts — call 600 52 5500 and the account will be unlocked after verification.

3. FAB ATM

A FAB ATM gives you your balance in under a minute. Insert your card, enter your PIN, and select Balance Inquiry. You can also request a mini-statement, which prints recent transactions. ATMs help when your phone has no battery or signal. Keep your PIN private and cover the keypad. Checks at FAB ATMs are free; a balance enquiry at another UAE bank's ATM costs AED 1 per transaction under FAB's official schedule of charges.

4. Phone banking

Phone banking works with no internet and no ATM nearby. Call FAB customer care on 600 52 5500 in the UAE, or +971 2 681 1511 from outside the UAE. Have your account details ready, and be ready to confirm your identity. Never share your PIN, OTP or password with anyone who calls you, even if they claim to be from FAB. If someone calls and asks for those details, hang up and call 600 52 5500 yourself.

5. SMS banking

SMS banking works from your registered mobile number: text BAL plus the last four digits of your account number to 2121. For a credit card, use a different keyword — CBAL plus the last four digits of the card number, also to 2121. If SMS does not work, your number may not be registered for SMS banking — call 600 52 5500. Never send your full account number, PIN or OTP by text.

6. Statements and mini-statements

A statement is a full record over a period, showing money in, money out and the balance at the end. A mini-statement is shorter — usually your most recent transactions — available at an ATM, in the app, or in online banking. Statements help when your question is not "what is my balance right now?" They show whether a payment went through or when your salary was credited. If a statement shows a transaction you do not recognise, call FAB on 600 52 5500.

Available balance vs ledger balance

Many people see two different figures and worry one is wrong. The ledger balance is the total in the account; the available balance is what you can actually spend right now, after holds such as a pending card payment or a cheque that has not cleared. If the two differ, the available balance is the one that matters for spending — and the transaction history in the app or online banking will show what is being held.
