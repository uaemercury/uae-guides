Prepaid Card Enquiry in the UAE — How to Check Your FAB Prepaid Card Balance

A prepaid card enquiry asks one simple question: how much money is on my card right now? A prepaid card is loaded with money in advance, and there is no bank account behind it. In the UAE, the most common prepaid cards are salary cards issued under the Wage Protection System, and First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB) is one of the main issuers. This guide explains what the enquiry is, which portal to use, and how to avoid scams.

What "prepaid card inquiry" means

"Prepaid card inquiry" and "prepaid card enquiry" mean the same thing: checking the balance on a prepaid card. A prepaid card is different from a debit card. A debit card is linked to a bank account. A prepaid card is loaded with money by an employer, and you can only spend what has been loaded. There is no chequebook and no overdraft. When the money runs out, the card stops working until it is loaded again.

FAB's prepaid salary cards are issued through employers under the UAE Wage Protection System (WPS), the electronic salary-transfer scheme supervised by MOHRE and the Central Bank of the UAE. The best-known FAB prepaid salary card is Ratibi, built for workers earning up to AED 5,000 per month. Other cards on the same system include the Lulu salary card and other WPS cards. The enquiry process is the same for all of them.

FAB itself was formed in April 2017, when First Gulf Bank and National Bank of Abu Dhabi (NBAD) merged. NBAD no longer exists as a separate bank. FAB is licensed and regulated by the Central Bank of the UAE, and its official website is bankfab.com — type it yourself, and never follow a balance-check link sent by SMS or email.

Which prepaid cards this covers

Ratibi is a FAB prepaid salary card. Your employer loads your salary onto it each pay cycle, and you withdraw cash at ATMs or pay in shops with it. It is not a bank account, so the FAB Mobile app may not show a balance for it. Use the PPC portal instead.

Some prepaid salary cards were issued before 2017 by the National Bank of Abu Dhabi (NBAD). NBAD merged with First Gulf Bank in April 2017 to form FAB, and old "Ratibi NBAD" cards carried over into FAB and Magnati. If you still hold one, check it on the current PPC portals below. The old PPC NBAD portal is decommissioned, and there is no separate NBAD online banking portal any more.

The Lulu salary card is another prepaid salary card used by some employers. The enquiry method is the same: the PPC portal, an ATM, or the Payit app. If you are unsure who issued your card, look at the name printed on it or ask your employer.

The fastest way: the PPC portals

The fastest way to check a prepaid card is the Prepaid Card Inquiry (PPC) portal. There are two official portals, and both work for FAB prepaid cards:

  • FAB portal: ppc.bankfab.com/PPCInquiry/
  • Magnati portal: ppc.magnati.com/ppc-inquiry/

Magnati is FAB's payments technology partner. No registration or app is needed. Type the address into your browser yourself. Never reach the portal by clicking a link in a text message or an email.

The fields requested vary by portal version. The Magnati version asks for the last 4 digits of the card, your Card ID, and a CAPTCHA. The FAB PPC version asks for the 16-digit card number and CVV.

The Card ID trap — the reason most checks fail

The most common reason a prepaid card enquiry fails is Card ID confusion. Your Card ID is not your card number. Your card number is the long 16-digit number on the front of the card. Your Card ID is a separate reference number printed on the card or on its welcome-envelope carrier — the paper the card arrived attached to. If you cannot find it, check the envelope your card arrived in, or ask your employer.

What FAB never asks for

The portal never asks for your PIN or OTP. FAB warns that it never asks for PIN, OTP or password via phone, SMS, email or WhatsApp. If any balance page asks for your PIN or OTP, close it at once. Never enter your PIN, OTP or CVV on any balance page you reached through a link in a message.

Two other ways to check

If you have no internet access or no smartphone, a FAB ATM works: insert the card, enter your PIN on the keypad, and select Balance Inquiry or a mini-statement. FAB ATMs are free for this check. A balance enquiry at another UAE bank's ATM costs AED 1 per transaction under FAB's official schedule of charges. Cover the keypad when you type your PIN. If the ATM keeps your card or shows an error, contact FAB on 600 52 5500 in the UAE, or +971 2 681 1511 from outside the UAE.

Payit is FAB's wallet app. Ratibi cardholders register in Payit as a "Ratibi Cardholder": download Payit, select "Ratibi Cardholder", scan your Emirates ID, confirm your details, then add your card ID and the last 2 digits of your card number, enter your mobile number, enter the 6-digit SMS code, and create a 4-digit PIN — then check the balance in the app. If Payit does not show the balance, fall back to the PPC portal.

If the balance looks wrong

First, check which card you looked up — many workers hold more than one. Second, check whether your salary has actually been loaded yet. The portal shows the balance on the card, not money your employer has promised but not sent. If your employer confirms the salary was loaded and the card still shows nothing, call FAB customer care on 600 52 5500.

SMS and WhatsApp: what to avoid

For a Ratibi balance, use the PPC portal, a FAB ATM, or the Payit app. FAB does not offer balance checks through WhatsApp. In 2026 the Central Bank of the UAE directed all UAE banks to stop using WhatsApp for customer communication and financial services. Never share your PIN, OTP, card number or Card ID with anyone on WhatsApp, and never send a photo of your card to anyone.
