FAB Salary Card Balance Check: How to See Your Salary Card Balance Online

If your employer pays you on a FAB salary card, your salary does not sit in a bank account — it is loaded onto a prepaid card. That one fact decides how you check the balance. The FAB Mobile app and online banking will not show it, because there is no account behind the card to log in to. The balance lives on a separate prepaid card portal, and checking it takes about a minute once you know which details it asks for.

What a FAB salary card actually is

A salary card is a prepaid card issued through an employer under the UAE Wage Protection System (WPS), the electronic salary-transfer scheme supervised by MOHRE and the Central Bank of the UAE. Your employer loads your salary onto the card each pay cycle. The card has no chequebook and no overdraft, and you do not open a bank account to get one — your employer arranges it.

The best-known FAB salary card is Ratibi, built for workers earning up to AED 5,000 per month. Other WPS prepaid cards work the same way, including Lulu salary cards. If you change jobs, your new employer may issue a new card, and each card has its own balance — so check you are looking at the right one.

FAB itself was formed in April 2017, when First Gulf Bank and National Bank of Abu Dhabi (NBAD) merged. NBAD no longer exists as a separate bank. FAB is licensed and regulated by the Central Bank of the UAE, and its official website is bankfab.com — type it yourself, and never follow a balance-check link sent by SMS or email.

Salary account vs salary card — the key difference

This is where most people go wrong. A salary account is a bank account: your salary is paid into it, and you check it in the FAB Mobile app or FAB Personal Online Banking, at an ATM, or by phone on 600 52 5500. A salary card is a prepaid card: your salary is loaded onto it, there is no bank account behind it, and you check it on the PPC portal, at an ATM, or in the Payit app.

The rule is simple: if you opened a FAB bank account, use the app. If your employer gave you a card and you never opened an account, use the PPC portal. If you hold both, check each separately — the app will not show the card balance, and the portal will not show the account balance.

The fastest way: the PPC portal

The Prepaid Card Inquiry (PPC) portal is the fastest online way to check a salary card balance. No registration and no app are needed. There are two portals:

  • FAB portal: ppc.bankfab.com/PPCInquiry/
  • Magnati portal: ppc.magnati.com/ppc-inquiry/

Magnati is FAB's payments technology partner. The old PPC NBAD portal is decommissioned, so any old bookmark for it will not work.

On the Magnati portal, the fields are the last 4 digits of your card number, your Card ID, and a CAPTCHA. The FAB portal asks for the details shown on its own page — enter only what the live page asks for, and complete the CAPTCHA if one is shown. Type the portal address yourself. Never click a portal link sent by SMS, email or WhatsApp, and never enter your PIN on a balance-check page — the portal asks for your Card ID, never your PIN.

The Card ID trap — the reason most checks fail

The most common reason a salary card balance check fails is Card ID confusion. Your Card ID is not your card number. Your card number is the long 16-digit number on the front of the card. Your Card ID is a separate reference number printed on the card itself or on its welcome-envelope carrier — the paper the card arrived attached to. If the portal rejects your details, check the Card ID first; most people type the card number into the Card ID field by mistake.

Two other ways to check

If you are out and about, a FAB ATM works: insert your card, enter your PIN, and select Balance Inquiry. Salary cardholders who use the Payit app can register as a "Ratibi Cardholder" there — you scan your Emirates ID, enter your card ID and the last 2 digits of the card, verify with the 6-digit SMS code, and set a 4-digit PIN. And if you would rather talk to someone, call FAB customer care on 600 52 5500 in the UAE.

When your salary has not arrived

Under the WPS rules in force since 1 June 2026, the 1st day of each Gregorian month is the unified salary due date for the preceding month's wages — payment after the 1st counts as delayed. So if your card shows no new salary a few days into the month, check with your employer first; they load the salary onto the card. If your employer confirms it was sent and the card still shows nothing, call FAB on 600 52 5500.
