Moving Out of Dubai? How Your DEWA Deposit Refund Actually Works

Moving out of a Dubai home comes with a long checklist, and the DEWA deposit is one item people often leave until the last minute. When you first connected electricity and water, DEWA took a refundable security deposit — AED 2,000 for a residential apartment, AED 4,000 for a villa, and AED 6,000 to 50,000 for commercial premises depending on the connected load. That money is yours. It sits with DEWA while your account is active, and it comes back when you close the connection properly — but only if you follow the right sequence.

Final bill first, refund second

The part most people misunderstand is the order. DEWA does not return your full deposit and then bill you separately. You submit a Move-Out (deactivation) request on dewa.gov.ae or the DEWA Smart App before you vacate. DEWA takes a final meter reading, generates your final electronic Green Bill — usually within 24 hours — deducts it from the deposit you paid, and refunds only the balance.

A simple example makes it clear: a AED 2,000 apartment deposit minus a AED 350 final bill leaves roughly AED 1,650 coming back to you. If the final bill is larger than the deposit, you pay the difference instead. That is why the move-out date matters — time it to your actual handover, so you are not billed for days after you have left.

How to submit the move-out request

Log in to dewa.gov.ae or the DEWA Smart App with your DEWA ID or UAE Pass, and go to Consumer, then Deactivation of Electricity and Water (Move-out). If you do not want to log in, there is an "Apply Online (without login)" route under Deactivation: enter your 10-digit contract account number and 9-digit premises number, then verify with the OTP sent to your registered mobile or email.

You will enter the contract account number of the account you are closing, choose your move-out date and time, and pick a refund method. Supply deactivates within 24 working hours of the selected time. You will also settle any outstanding balance shown, plus the deactivation fee — AED 100 for a small meter or AED 300 for a large meter, plus AED 10 knowledge fee and AED 10 innovation fee. Once submitted, you get a move-out notification number by email and SMS; keep it for any follow-up.

Which refund method is fastest

IBAN bank transfer is the fastest route. Automated refunds up to AED 4,000 now process in as little as 8 to 30 minutes, provided the account is in your name and your registered phone and email are current. Western Union works the same day and is the right choice if you have already left the UAE — you receive a Money Transfer Control Number valid for 30 days, usable at any branch worldwide. A cheque is the slowest option, collected or received by post. If you are moving to another home in Dubai, transferring the balance to your other active DEWA account is fast too — and pairs well with DEWA's Move-To service, which can shift your account and, where eligible, your deposit to the new address instead of closing one account and paying a second full deposit. Larger deposits and cheque refunds may take 3 to 7 working days for manual processing.

Two things to save before you leave

After the final bill is settled, DEWA automatically emails you a clearance certificate confirming the account is closed with nothing outstanding. Save it — landlords, property managers and tenancy-cancellation paperwork often ask for it. And if you are leaving the UAE permanently, submit the move-out request before your flight, coordinate it with your Ejari cancellation and lease end date, and choose Western Union if your UAE bank account is already closed — sorting an IBAN refund to a closed account takes extra correspondence.
