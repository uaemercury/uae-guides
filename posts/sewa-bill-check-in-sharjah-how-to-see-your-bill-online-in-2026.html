SEWA Bill Check in Sharjah: How to See Your Bill Online in 2026

If you live in Sharjah, your electricity, water and gas all arrive on one bill — from SEWA, the Sharjah Electricity, Water and Gas Authority. SEWA has served the emirate since 1995, covering more than 2 million residents, and unlike DEWA in Dubai, its bills include piped natural gas alongside electricity and water. The good news is that checking the bill takes less than a minute, and you do not need an account or a login to do it.

Check your bill on sewa.gov.ae (no login)

The fastest route is the official website. Go to sewa.gov.ae and open Bill Inquiry or Check Bill — sometimes listed under E-Services or Billing Services. Enter your account number or consumer number, which is printed on any previous bill, usually at the top-right. Click Next or Submit, and your current bill appears: the amount due, the due date and a usage breakdown. From there you can download the bill as a PDF or pay by card on the spot.

No username or password is required for a basic bill check. Registering is only worthwhile if you want bill history across several months or you manage more than one account.

Where to find your consumer number

Your consumer number is the identifier printed on every SEWA bill, at the top-right. Past payment receipts carry it too. If you have lost it entirely, call SEWA on 600 566665 with your Emirates ID — the agent can retrieve both your account and consumer numbers for you.

Three other ways to check

Not near a computer? The SEWA Smart App, available on the App Store and Google Play, lets you register or log in, add your consumer account and open "My bills" — it also handles payments and service requests. By SMS, send your customer or account number to 7011 and the bill details arrive within minutes, with no app and no login needed. Or call 600 566665 and give the agent your account number for your current bill and balance.

Paying the bill

Once you know the amount, Quick Pay on sewa.gov.ae takes a card payment with just the account number — no login — which is handy if you are paying a relative's bill. The SEWA Smart App, participating UAE bank apps and ATMs, payment kiosks across Sharjah (with instant receipts), Customer Happiness Centres and exchange houses all work too. Card payments through Quick Pay reflect quickly; bank or ATM payments can take up to a day, so keep your confirmation and wait 24 hours before chasing a payment that has not shown up.

If the bill does not appear, or looks wrong

The most common reason a bill will not load is a mistyped number — re-enter the consumer or account number exactly as printed, since an extra space is usually the culprit. If it still fails, call 600 566665 to verify the number on file. If the amount looks wrong, compare the usage breakdown with previous months rather than the dirham total; registered users can raise a dispute under Billing & Payments, or call customer care. And remember that SEWA tariffs differ from DEWA's slab system, so always check the current tariff schedule on sewa.gov.ae before disputing a charge.

One more distinction worth knowing: Ajman and most other northern emirates fall under FEWA, not SEWA — only Sharjah and some surrounding areas use SEWA. If you have just moved to Sharjah, a new connection needs a tenancy contract registered with Sharjah Municipality, your Emirates ID and the premises details, with a refundable security deposit whose amount varies by property type.
