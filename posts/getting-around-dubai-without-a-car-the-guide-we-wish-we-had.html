Getting Around Dubai Without a Car: The Guide We Wish We Had

Plenty of people land in Dubai assuming a car is non-negotiable. It is not. Between the Metro, the bus network and a Nol card that costs less than a coffee, the city is genuinely easy to cross without driving — once you know how the pieces fit together.

Start with a Nol card

The Nol card is your key to the Metro, buses, trams and even some parking meters. Pick one up at any Metro station, top it up at machines or via the app, and tap in and out. Our main site keeps a full, updated walkthrough of Nol card balance check options so you never get caught short at the gate.

Metro first, bus for the last mile

The Red and Green lines cover most of what a newcomer needs: the airport, Deira, Bur Dubai, Downtown and the Marina corridor. Buses fill the gaps between stations and neighbourhoods. Plan the day around prayer and peak hours and the system feels effortless — our companion guide to shopping around prayer times shows how residents time errands between the two.

The honest verdict

Give it two weeks. Most residents who try the Metro-plus-bus combo for their commute and weekly shop quietly stop missing the car — and the parking hunt that comes with it.
