Ratibi Card Balance Check: How to See Your FAB Salary Card Balance Online

If you are paid on a Ratibi card in the UAE, your salary does not sit in a bank account — it is loaded onto a prepaid card. That changes how you check the balance. The FAB Mobile app and online banking will not show it, because there is no account behind the card to log in to. Instead, you use a separate prepaid card portal, and it takes about a minute once you know which details it asks for.

What a Ratibi card actually is

Ratibi is a prepaid salary card from First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB), issued through employers under the UAE Wage Protection System (WPS) — the electronic salary-transfer scheme supervised by MOHRE and the Central Bank of the UAE. It is built for workers earning up to AED 5,000 per month, and it has no chequebook and no overdraft. Your employer arranges the card; you do not open a bank account to get one.

FAB itself was formed in April 2017, when First Gulf Bank and National Bank of Abu Dhabi (NBAD) merged. Old "Ratibi NBAD" cards carried over into FAB and Magnati — Magnati is FAB's payments technology partner — after the merger. The old PPC NBAD portal is decommissioned, so any old bookmark for it will not work.

The fastest way: the PPC portal

The Prepaid Card Inquiry (PPC) portal is the fastest way to check a Ratibi balance online. No registration and no app are needed. There are two portals:

  • FAB portal: ppc.bankfab.com/PPCInquiry/
  • Magnati portal: ppc.magnati.com/ppc-inquiry/

On the Magnati portal, the fields are the last 4 digits of your card number, your Card ID, and a CAPTCHA. The FAB portal asks for the details shown on its own page — enter only what the live page asks for, and complete the CAPTCHA if one is shown.

Type the portal address yourself. Never click a portal link sent by SMS, email or WhatsApp, and never enter your PIN on a balance-check page — the portal asks for your Card ID, never your PIN.

The Card ID trap — the reason most checks fail

The most common reason a Ratibi balance check fails is Card ID confusion. Your Card ID is not your card number. Your card number is the long 16-digit number on the front of the card. Your Card ID is a separate reference number printed on the card itself or on its welcome-envelope carrier — the paper the card arrived attached to. If the portal rejects your details, check the Card ID first; most people type the card number into the Card ID field by mistake.

Three other ways to check

If you are out and about, a FAB ATM works: insert your card, enter your PIN, and select Balance Inquiry. Ratibi cardholders who use the Payit app can register as a "Ratibi Cardholder" there — you scan your Emirates ID, enter your card ID and the last 2 digits of the card, verify with the 6-digit SMS code, and set a 4-digit PIN. And if you would rather talk to someone, call FAB customer care on 600 52 5500 in the UAE.

When your salary has not arrived

Under the WPS rules in force since 1 June 2026, the 1st day of each Gregorian month is the unified salary due date for the preceding month's wages — payment after the 1st counts as delayed. So if your card shows no new salary a few days into the month, check with your employer first; they load the salary onto the card. If your employer confirms it was sent and the card still shows nothing, call FAB on 600 52 5500.

If you change jobs, your new employer may issue a new card — ask them if you are unsure which card your salary goes to, because each card has its own balance on the portal.
