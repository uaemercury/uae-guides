SEWA Bill Calculator: Estimate Your Sharjah Electricity, Water and Gas Bill in 2026

If you live in Sharjah, your electricity, water and gas all come from one authority — SEWA, the Sharjah Electricity, Water and Gas Authority — and they all arrive on one bill. The trouble is that the bill itself only tells you what you owe after the month is over. If you want to know roughly what next month will cost before it arrives, an estimate tool does the job in under a minute.

What the calculator asks for

The tool needs just three numbers: your monthly electricity usage in kWh, your water usage in imperial gallons, and your gas usage in cubic metres. You will find all three on any previous SEWA bill, or you can read them straight off your meters. It also asks for your resident type — expat or UAE national — because SEWA's tariffs differ by category.

That is all. There is no sign-up, no account, and nothing to download. The calculator runs in your browser and is completely free.

How the estimate is worked out

Electricity is charged on SEWA's residential slab tariffs, so higher usage pushes you into higher-priced slabs. For expats, the published 2026 electricity slabs run at 28, 35 and 45 fils per kWh. Water works the same way on slabs: for expats, 4.5, 5.5 and 6.0 fils per gallon as consumption rises. Sewerage is charged at 1.5 fils per gallon for expats, and gas at around AED 2.15 per cubic metre.

On top of the usage charges, the estimate adds the fixed monthly service charges for your electricity, water and gas connections, plus the applicable fuel surcharges and municipality fees, and 5% VAT. That is why the result is close to a real bill rather than a bare usage total.

What it cannot do

The calculator is built for residential customers in Sharjah. Commercial and industrial premises use different SEWA tariff categories, so the estimate may not apply to them. And it is an estimate, not a promise: your actual bill can differ slightly because of surcharges, fees or a tariff change. Tariffs can change, so always confirm against your official SEWA bill at sewa.gov.ae.

Used the right way, though, it is genuinely useful. Run it before summer, when air conditioning pushes your kWh up, and you can see the cost of the next slab before the bill lands. Run it after a high bill and you can work out whether the jump came from electricity, water or gas.

For the full walkthrough — how each slab works, what the fixed charges cover, and the answers to the common questions — try the free SEWA bill calculator for Sharjah. Enter your three usage numbers and it gives you an instant estimate of your monthly bill.
