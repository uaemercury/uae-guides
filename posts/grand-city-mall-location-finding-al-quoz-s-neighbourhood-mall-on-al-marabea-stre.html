Grand City Mall Location: Finding Al Quoz's Neighbourhood Mall on Al Marabea' Street

Ask for Grand City Mall and a search engine may send you to the wrong emirate — there is a Grand Mall in Ajman and another in Sharjah. The Dubai one, the subject of this guide, stands at No. 4 Al Marabea' Street in Al Quoz Industrial Area 4, in the middle of the city's workshop-and-warehouse district. That location is not an accident or a compromise. It is the mall's founding idea: put the weekly shop, the pharmacy, the exchange and the typing centre where the people who need them already are. Here is how to reach it by every kind of transport, and what you will find around it when you arrive.

The Address and the Area

Al Quoz Industrial Area 4 is the south-eastern quarter of Al Quoz, close to Al Khail Road — a district of warehouses, workshops, showrooms and labour accommodation, threaded with the services that support them. Grand City Mall sits on Al Marabea' Street among them, anchored by Grand Hypermarket at ground floor. Its catchment runs in two directions at once: the huge workforce of the industrial area on its doorstep, and the villa communities of Al Barsha 2, Al Safa 2 and Umm Al Sheif a short drive away. That dual catchment explains the tenant mix — value groceries and daily services for the workers, practical shops for the families.

For sat-nav or ride-hailing, "Grand City Mall, Al Marabea' Street, Al Quoz Industrial Area 4" lands you at the door. The mall's number is +971 4 341 0105 if you want to confirm anything before travelling.

By Car: Four Approaches

Most visitors drive, and the road network cooperates from every side. From Sheikh Zayed Road, exit towards Al Quoz and follow the signs into the industrial area. From Al Khail Road (E44), the Al Quoz exits drop you close to Al Marabea' Street. Latifa Bint Hamdan Street and Al Asayel Street are the local approaches serving Al Barsha and Al Safa. From the villa communities, the drive is short enough that families treat this as their weekly-shop mall in preference to the Mall of the Emirates crowds up the road.

The reward at the end of the drive is free parking — shaded and open bays, 400+ spaces, no ticket and no validation. The one caution is Friday evening, the week's peak, when the car park fills. Taxis and ride-hailing cars use the Al Marabea' Street frontage for drop-off and pick-up around the clock.

By Metro: Equiti, Then a Short Ride

Al Quoz has no metro station inside the industrial area, so a metro journey always ends with a short last leg. The closest station is Equiti on the Red Line — the station formerly known as Al Quoz — and ONPASSIVE station, also on the Red Line, is the other nearby option. From either, a short taxi or ride-hailing ride finishes the trip; a connecting bus works too. It is only a few minutes by road, but it is not a walk to attempt with shopping bags in summer.

For cross-city commuters this metro-plus-taxi pattern is the standard commute, and it pairs well with the mall's 7:00 AM opening — the connection works even for early shifts.

By Bus: How Al Quoz Actually Moves

The bus is the district's real transport, and the mall is well served: routes 21, 84, 96, F25 and F26 pass near the mall on and around Al Marabea' Street. The feeder routes — F25 and F26 — are designed to connect neighbourhoods like this one with the Red Line, which makes the bus a genuine alternative to the taxi for the last leg. Stops sit within a short walk of the entrances. Check the RTA's current schedules for the day you travel, since route details change from time to time.

What Is Around It

The mall does not stand alone. Al Quoz Mall is about seven minutes away, Al Khail Gate Community Centre around eleven, and Alserkal Avenue — the famous arts quarter of warehouse galleries — roughly twelve minutes by road, with Quoz Pond Park a few minutes further. That makes an easy pairing: practical errands at Grand City Mall, then coffee and galleries on Alserkal. Mall of the Emirates is the big leisure alternative up Sheikh Zayed Road — but for the weekly routine, the neighbourhood keeps coming back to Al Marabea' Street.

Landmarks and Driving Tips

A few orientation points help first-timers. The mall is the building anchored by the Grand Hypermarket signage on Al Marabea' Street — the hypermarket frontage is the landmark drivers actually navigate by. Al Quoz's street grid is numbered and logical once you are in it, but the industrial area's blocks are large, so the last few hundred metres are best done with the map open or the mall's name in the ride-hailing app. Traffic follows the district's working day: busy at shift changes and around Friday prayers, calm in the mid-morning and again late in the evening, when the mall still has hours left to run.

If you are combining the trip with the rest of Al Quoz — a showroom visit, a workshop errand, a gallery on Alserkal Avenue — the mall makes a natural base: park free, finish the practical jobs, then move on. That base-camp role, as much as any single store, is what its location really provides.
