Gas Bill Check in Dubai — How to See and Pay Your Gas Bill in 2026

If your gas bill in Dubai feels harder to find than your DEWA bill, there is a reason. Your gas bill is not part of your DEWA bill at all.

It is a separate bill from a separate company. Two companies supply piped gas in Dubai — Lootah BC Gas and Emirates Gas — so how you check and pay depends on which one supplies your building.

Which supplier serves your building?

There is no single gas portal for the whole of Dubai. The first step is always to find out which company supplies your building.

In older parts of Dubai, Lootah BC Gas is the more common supplier. In newer areas, Emirates Gas is more common. The only way to be certain is your bill: the supplier is printed on the gas bill itself and on the meter.

In some buildings, cooking gas is charged through the DEWA bill as part of the housing fee. If that is your case, there is no separate gas bill to check. The gas charge sits inside your DEWA bill, and the DEWA deposit refund and move-out rules apply to it.

Lootah BC Gas: check and pay without logging in

If your supplier is Lootah BC Gas, use the Lootah customer portal at apps.lootahbcgas.com. It offers Quick Pay, which needs no login — only your 15-digit account number.

Open the portal, click Quick Pay, enter your 15-digit account number, and you will see your bill with the amount and the due date. Then pay by credit card. Your account number is printed on your gas bill.

Emirates Gas customers

If your supplier is Emirates Gas, pay through the company's website at emiratesgas.com and follow the payment instructions there. If you cannot find the payment page, check the payment details printed on your bill.

Pay in the DubaiNow app

Lootah BC Gas customers can also view and pay in the DubaiNow app. Digital Dubai launched gas billing on DubaiNow with Lootah BC Gas in June 2026 — the first gas billing service on the app.

Other ways to pay Lootah BC Gas

Beyond the portal, Lootah offers bank transfer to its Dubai Islamic Bank account. The account number and IBAN for your community are on Lootah's Payment Options page.

You can also use MBME kiosks, pay in person at the Deira service centre, use Al Ansari Exchange branches, or pay the meter reader directly.

Moving home? Check the gas type first

If you move to a building with central piped gas, you will get a new gas account. If you move to a building with cylinder supply, you will need to arrange a separate LPG supply. Check before you move, not after.

How much is the average gas bill?

There is no single average gas bill. The amount depends on the size of your home, the number of people, how much gas you use, and the tariff for your building. If you do not know the rate, check your bill.

Add your gas bill and your DEWA bill together to get your total monthly utility cost. A gas bill is often much lower than a DEWA bill — the two are separate bills.

What happens if you miss a payment?

Both suppliers can charge late fees or, in serious cases, cut off the gas supply. Pay before the due date and do not wait until it is disconnected. Keep your payment receipt until the payment shows on your account.
