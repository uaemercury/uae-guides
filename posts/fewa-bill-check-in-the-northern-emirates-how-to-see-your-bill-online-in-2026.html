FEWA Bill Check in the Northern Emirates — How to See Your Bill Online in 2026

If you live in Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah or Fujairah, your electricity and water bill does not come from DEWA. It comes from FEWA — now called Etihad Water and Electricity (EtihadWE).

The good news is that checking it takes about three minutes, and you do not need to log in.

What is FEWA?

FEWA is now called Etihad Water and Electricity (EtihadWE). The name changed in 2020, when the Federal Electricity and Water Authority was established under Federal Law No. 31 of 2020. It is wholly owned by the Emirates Investment Authority.

EtihadWE is the federal utility set up by the UAE Government to supply electricity and water in the Northern Emirates — Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah. It also covers Al Dhaid and Dibba Al Fujairah.

Its job is to deliver power and water in those emirates in line with international standards.

How to check your FEWA bill online (no login needed)

The fastest way is the official EtihadWE website. Its "View and Pay" service lets you look up your bill without logging into an account. The steps are simple.

Open the official EtihadWE website. From the main menu, go to the "Services" tab and select "View and Pay". Enter your account number. Your current bill will appear on screen, showing the payable amount, the consumption, the service charges and the tax.

You can then pay it from the same screen with a GCC-issued credit card. Keep a screenshot of the successful transaction or the confirmation message you receive. Once the payment is done, the website shows the transaction details.

What you need before you start

All you need to check the bill is your account number. To pay, you also need a valid credit card and an email address. The card must be issued in the GCC region.

A few limits apply to card payments. The maximum credit card payment is AED 10,000.00, and a fee of 0.0068 applies to amounts above AED 10,000.00. You can make up to 5 credit card transactions per day.

The official page does not state whether the 0.0068 fee is a percentage, a rate or a flat amount. Do not try to work out a surcharge from it — the amount the portal quotes at checkout is the amount that applies.

The service code is 357-04-002-000, and the expected completion time is 1 working day.

Other ways to pay your FEWA bill

The mobile app is the next easiest option. You can pay through the EtihadWE mobile app by logging in with UAE PASS or proceeding through the available payment option, selecting the account and completing the transaction.

Payment kiosks are available around the clock and take about 3 minutes. Banks are also available around the clock for online payments, and the process takes about 3 minutes. Al Ansari Exchange is open from Saturday to Thursday, and paying there takes about 10 minutes.

You can also visit an EtihadWE service centre during official working hours (about 10 minutes). Centres are in Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Al Dhaid, Fujairah, Dibba Al Fujairah and Ras Al Khaimah.

FEWA vs DEWA vs SEWA — which one is yours?

The three utilities serve different emirates, and the process is similar for all of them: enter the account number on the official site and the bill appears. FEWA (EtihadWE) supplies the Northern Emirates. DEWA supplies Dubai. SEWA supplies Sharjah.

If you live in Dubai, your bill is with DEWA. If you live in Sharjah, it is with SEWA. If you live in the Northern Emirates, it is with FEWA. Check your bill with the supplier that serves your emirate.

If the amount on your bill looks higher than usual, check the meter reading, the consumption and the billed period before assuming an error. A longer billing period or higher usage explains most sudden jumps.

Stay on the official site

Only pay through official EtihadWE channels. A fake FEWA website can steal your card details, so always check that you are on the official etihadwe.ae domain before entering anything.
