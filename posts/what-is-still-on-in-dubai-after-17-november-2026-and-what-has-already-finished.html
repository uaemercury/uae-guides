UAE MERCURY — Your Window to the Emirates

First, the trap: half of the "November in Dubai" lists circulating right now are advertising events that have already finished. Design Week is over. The Diwali programme is over. The DP World Tour Championship ended on 15 November. If you are reading this on or after 17 November 2026, your real calendar is the second half of the month — and it is dense enough that you do not need the leftovers. Here is what is genuinely still on, with the finished events marked as finished.

Already happened — do not plan around these

Dubai Design Week ran 3–8 November. Diwali and the Festival of Lights ran 2–12 November, including the Noor evenings at Souk Al Seef on 7–8 November, and no free Diwali fireworks were announced for 2026. Dubai Ride took place on 1 November and Dubai Stand Up Paddle on 7–8 November, both part of the Fitness Challenge. Concerts by JONY (4 November), Amr Diab and Ministry of Sound Classical (both 7 November) are done, as are the Emaar Dubai Premier Padel P1 (8–15 November), Arabian Soul Fest (14–15 November) and the DP World Tour Championship at Jumeirah Golf Estates (12–15 November). Anything listing those as upcoming is out of date.

Still on: the Fitness Challenge and Dubai Run

The Dubai Fitness Challenge — the 10th edition of the 30x30, 30 minutes of activity a day for 30 days — runs citywide until 29 November 2026, and joining is free, with free classes and fitness villages across the city. Its biggest remaining event is Dubai Run on Sunday 22 November: a 6:30am start on Sheikh Zayed Road, a 5km Downtown route and a 10km route, both starting near the Museum of the Future. The start line closes at 8:00am and the road reopens at 9:00am. Entry is free but registration is compulsory, with no sign-up on the day, and bibs and T-shirts are collected at the Dubai Municipality 30x30 Fitness Village in Zabeel Park between 9 and 21 November. The challenge ends with Dubai Yoga, free, on 29 November. Note the knock-on effect: Sheikh Zayed Road closes until 9am on the 22nd, so plan any cross-city trip that morning around it.

Still on: the Dubai 7s and the concert run

The Emirates Dubai 7s runs 27–29 November at The Sevens Stadium on the Dubai–Al Ain Road, as the opening round of the HSBC SVNS series. Friday entry starts at AED 75 with under-15s free that day; Saturday and Sunday day passes are AED 455, a three-day pass starts at AED 595, and a three-day family pass is AED 1,136. Akon headlines on Saturday 28 November, Chase & Status close on Sunday 29 November, and Melanie C and Jax Jones also play. Sunday 29 November was reported to have limited spaces left. The concert calendar around it is heavy: Trevor Noah at Dubai Opera on 25, 26, 28 and 29 November from AED 295, with the first two nights reported sold out; Atif Aslam at Coca-Cola Arena on 27 November at 9pm; Hiba Tawaji and Ibrahim Maalouf at Dubai Opera the same night; Sid Sriram at Coca-Cola Arena on 22 November; Anuv Jain at Expo City on 20 November; KEINEMUSIK at Bab Al Shams Arena and MK live on 21 November; Boris Brejcha at Soho Garden DXB on 27 November; and Marco Carola at FIVE Luxe JBR on 20 November.

Still on: shows, trade fairs and Global Village

World Art Dubai runs 19–22 November at the Dubai World Trade Centre from AED 20, with more than 10,000 artworks, over 300 exhibitors and 65 countries represented. The Dubai International Boat Show runs 25–29 November at Dubai Harbour from AED 60, with more than 200 boats on display. Big 5 Global, the construction trade show, runs 23–26 November at the World Trade Centre — register online in advance. Icons of Porsche runs 21–22 November at The Square, Nad Al Sheba, at AED 85 per day with under-12s free. Ultra Trail Dubai runs 27–29 November at Hatta Wadi Hub, with races from 5km to 100km. And Global Village Season 31 is open every evening until May 2027: 4pm to midnight Sunday to Wednesday, 1am Thursday to Saturday, AED 25 on weekdays and AED 30 any-day, with Friday and Saturday fireworks at 9pm. Tuesdays are Family Day, restricted to families, women and couples.

Use the live calendar, not a screenshot

Dates, prices and line-ups were checked against official and organiser sources on 17 November 2026, and they can still change — the official event page is the final word. For a season calendar that separates what is still on from what has finished, the Dubai events calendar on the UAE Mercury cloud hub is the cleaner starting point.

Weather is on your side for all of it: daytime highs around 30–31°C falling to about 29°C late in the month, nights at 18–22°C, roughly 10 hours of sunshine and a sea still warm at 27–29°C. Pack light clothes and one light jacket for desert evenings.
