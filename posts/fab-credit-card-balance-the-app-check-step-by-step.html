UAE MERCURY — Your Window to the Emirates

Checking a credit card balance should take seconds, and with the FAB Mobile app it does — once you know which screen holds which number. FAB (First Abu Dhabi Bank) shows three figures for every credit card, and this walkthrough takes you to them step by step, then explains what to do if your card does not appear.

Before you start

You need the FAB Mobile app installed (App Store, Google Play or Huawei AppGallery) and your online profile active. If you have never used the app, registration asks for your Emirates ID and card or account details — the app itself walks you through it, and FAB's own pages describe signing up with just an Emirates ID.

The steps

  1. Open FAB Mobile and sign in. Use your password, fingerprint or face — biometric login is supported and is the fastest route in.
  2. Look at the home screen. Account and card balances are shown here after login; for many users the card's key figure is already visible without tapping further.
  3. Tap Cards and select your credit card. This opens the card's own view.
  4. Read the three numbers. You will see your outstanding balance (everything you currently owe), your available credit (how much of your limit remains), and your minimum due with its due date.
  5. Tap Transactions to see recent spending with logos and categories, or open the statement view for the full month — useful when a number looks wrong and you want to find the charge behind it.

If your card is missing from the app

A card that does not appear is usually a linking issue, not a lost card. Cards that were only just issued, and supplementary cards, sometimes need to be linked to your profile first. Call FAB customer care on 600 52 5500 inside the UAE (or +971 2 681 1511 from abroad) and ask them to link it — do not try to register a second profile.

A two-minute weekly habit

The balance screen is also your fraud alarm. Skim the transaction list once a week: small unfamiliar charges are how card fraud usually starts, and FAB's real-time alerts make them easy to catch early. One warning worth memorising: FAB never asks for your PIN, password or OTP by phone, SMS, email or WhatsApp. Anyone who does is not the bank.

Thirty seconds in the app, three numbers understood, and your card never surprises you at a checkout again.

A note on available vs outstanding in the app

New cardholders often read the app's biggest number as "what I can spend" — it is the opposite. The figure labelled available credit is your spending room; the outstanding balance is your debt. If the two ever look identical, you have either a brand-new card or a fully used one, and the transactions tab will tell you which within seconds. Build the habit of opening the card view after any large purchase: watching the available credit fall in real time is the simplest budgeting teacher a cardholder gets, and it costs nothing but a glance.

And keep the location of the Cards screen memorised — home screen, Cards, your card — because the best balance check is the one you can perform half-asleep, in a taxi, with the driver waiting. Seconds matter to habits, and habits are what keep a credit card boring.
