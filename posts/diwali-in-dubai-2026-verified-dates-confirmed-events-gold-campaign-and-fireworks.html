Diwali in Dubai 2026: Verified Dates, Confirmed Events, Gold Campaign and Fireworks Status

A fact-first briefing on Diwali in Dubai 2026. Verified dates: Dhanteras 6 November, main Diwali (Lakshmi Puja) Sunday 8 November, Bhai Dooj 10 November; DFRE Festival of Lights 2-12 November; Noor: Festival of Lights at Souk Al Seef 6-8 November; Dubai Jewellery Group campaign 24 October-12 November with 1kg gold raffles. Also covered: the Jebel Ali temple relocation that older guides miss, the fireworks situation (no official 2026 schedule published yet), and the not-a-public-holiday rule.

The Five Days of Diwali in 2026

Diwali is not a single evening; it is a five-day festival, and in 2026 the sequence runs like this:

  • Dhanteras — Friday 6 November. The first diya is lit, and it is the traditional day for buying gold and metal.
  • Choti Diwali — Saturday 7 November. "Small Diwali": homes are decorated and sweets are exchanged.
  • Main Diwali (Lakshmi Puja) — Sunday 8 November. The main festival day, with Lakshmi Puja performed in the evening after sunset.
  • Govardhan Puja / Annakut — Monday 9 November. Marked with Annakut offerings at temples.
  • Bhai Dooj — Tuesday 10 November. The siblings' day that closes the five days.

Wrapped around the festival, Dubai's official DFRE Festival of Lights programme runs 2-12 November 2026 (per the Visit Dubai listing), so the city starts celebrating before Dhanteras and keeps going after Bhai Dooj.

Confirmed Events: Noor at Al Seef and More

The heritage heart of the celebrations is Noor: Festival of Lights at Souk Al Seef, 6-8 November, presented with DFRE and the Consulate General of India in Dubai — processions, storytelling, dance, live music, workshops, stand-up comedy and fireworks over Dubai Creek. On the main day itself, Diwali in the Park at JLT Park Amphitheatre (Sunday 8 November, 5pm-9pm) is a free community celebration that suits families perfectly.

Beyond the headline events, Dubai Holding communities — Al Furjan, JVC, JVT, Remraam, Serena, Discovery Gardens and others — run their own Diwali events with rangoli, lanterns and cultural performances, and malls across the city add decor, pop-up bazaars and dance shows. The Global Village Angle

One of the easiest ways to do Diwali night in Dubai is Global Village. Its India Pavilion serves Diwali food and hosts Bollywood entertainment through the season, and the park has staged special Diwali musical fireworks in past years (it runs regular 9pm fireworks in season; the 2026 Diwali display is expected rather than confirmed at the time of writing). If you are planning a visit during the festival window, this Global Village Season 31 guide covers this season's ticket prices, timings and bus routes.

Fireworks: What Is Confirmed and What Is Expected

Be sceptical of any guide that gives you exact 2026 fireworks times today: no official schedule had been published when this article was written. The expected spots, based on past editions, are Al Seef during the Noor Festival nights (6-8 November), Global Village, and Dubai Festival City with a Diwali-themed IMAGINE show; Downtown has shown Diwali visuals on Burj Khalifa before, though fireworks are never launched from the tower itself. And one firm rule for residents: personal fireworks and firecrackers are prohibited in the UAE — only licensed displays are allowed.

Gold: The 1kg Raffle and Dhanteras Shopping

Diwali is also Dubai's biggest gold moment. The Dubai Jewellery Group's campaign runs 24 October - 12 November 2026 at participating jewellers, with festive offers and raffles totalling 1kg of gold. Dhanteras (6 November) is the most auspicious buying day of the year, and with 24K gold having dipped below Dh500 per gram in mid-October reporting, festive buying started early. Prices move daily, so check the day's rate before heading out — and expect the Gold Souk in Deira and the jewellers of Meena Bazaar in Bur Dubai to be at their busiest on Dhanteras and the Diwali weekend.

Temples and Neighbourhoods

Dubai's main Hindu temple is now the Hindu Temple Dubai in Jebel Ali (in the Worship Village), opened in October 2022 and home to 16 deities. The old Bur Dubai Shiva Mandir relocated there in January 2024; the Krishna Mandir remains at the original Bur Dubai complex near the Dubai Museum. Both see very large Diwali crowds, extended festival hours and, on peak days, online registration or QR entry — check before you go and arrive early. For atmosphere, nothing beats Bur Dubai and Meena Bazaar in the evenings: balconies strung with lights, rangoli at doorways, and mithai shops doing their busiest weekend of the year.

Sweets, Bazaars and the Mall Scene

No Diwali in Dubai is complete without mithai. Sweet shops in Meena Bazaar and across Bur Dubai do their busiest trade of the year across the festival weekend, with gift boxes of laddoos, barfi and jalebi moving as fast as they can be packed — sweets are exchanged throughout the five days, starting with Choti Diwali. The malls join in with Diwali decor, pop-up bazaars and dance performances, which makes even a routine shopping trip feel like part of the festival. If you are visiting friends, a box of mithai or dates is the classic gift; arrive hungry either way.

Finally, a practical note: Diwali is not an official UAE public holiday. In 2026 the main day falls on a Sunday — the weekend for most UAE workers — and some private companies and Indian-curriculum schools grant leave by their own policy.
