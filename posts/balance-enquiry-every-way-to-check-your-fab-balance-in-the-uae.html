Balance Enquiry: Every Way to Check Your FAB Balance in the UAE

A balance enquiry asks one simple question: how much money do I have right now? For First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB) customers in the UAE, the answer is never more than a minute away — but the right method depends on what you hold, a bank account or a prepaid salary card, and where you are when you ask. This guide puts every channel in one place, so you can pick the fastest one for your situation.

What a balance enquiry actually means

The answer you get is usually your available balance — the money you can spend or withdraw now. Some channels also show a ledger balance, the total including amounts still being processed. A balance enquiry is not a statement. A statement is a full record over a period, showing money in, money out and the balance at the end. A mini-statement is shorter — usually your most recent transactions. An enquiry gives you a figure; a statement gives you the story behind it. If you want to know whether you can afford a purchase, do an enquiry. If you want to know where your money went, check a statement.

FAB was formed in April 2017, when First Gulf Bank and National Bank of Abu Dhabi (NBAD) merged. NBAD no longer exists as a separate bank. FAB is licensed and regulated by the Central Bank of the UAE, and its official website is bankfab.com — type it yourself, and never follow a balance-check link sent by SMS or email.

Every channel, in one place

FAB Mobile app — fastest for account holders. On the App Store, Google Play and Huawei AppGallery. After login, balances appear on the home screen. The app also manages accounts and cards, shows transactions with logos, categories and smart filters, and sends real-time alerts.

FAB Online Banking — best on a computer. At bankfab.com, sign in and open the Accounts view. You can also view and download statements there. If you enter the wrong password three times, your online banking account locks — call FAB customer care and it will be unlocked after verification.

FAB ATM — fastest when you are out. Insert your card, enter your PIN, and select Balance Inquiry. The ATM shows your balance, and you can request a mini-statement. Checks at FAB ATMs are free; other banks' ATMs may charge a small fee.

Phone banking — no internet needed. Call 600 52 5500 in the UAE, or +971 2 681 1511 from outside the UAE. Those numbers were verified on bankfab.com.

SMS — no smartphone needed. Text BAL followed by the last 4 digits of your account number to 2121. For a credit card, the keyword is different: CBAL followed by the last 4 digits of your card number to 2121. Do not mix the two up — BAL is for accounts, CBAL is for cards.

PPC portal — for prepaid salary cards only. If you hold a Ratibi or other WPS prepaid salary card, the app and online banking will not show it, because there is no account behind the card. Use the prepaid card portal instead: ppc.bankfab.com/PPCInquiry/ or ppc.magnati.com/ppc-inquiry/ — no registration or app is needed. On the Magnati portal the fields are the last 4 digits of your card number, your Card ID, and a CAPTCHA. Your Card ID is a separate reference number printed on the card or its welcome-envelope carrier — it is not your card number.

Payit app — FAB's wallet app. Ratibi cardholders can register in Payit as a "Ratibi Cardholder": scan your Emirates ID, enter your card ID and the last 2 digits of the card, verify with the 6-digit SMS code, and set a 4-digit PIN.

The fastest method for your situation

  • At home, with your phone: the FAB Mobile app — balances on the home screen after login.
  • On a computer: FAB Online Banking at bankfab.com.
  • Out with no internet: a FAB ATM, or SMS to 2121 if you know your last 4 digits.
  • Abroad: the app or online banking; by phone, call +971 2 681 1511.
  • No smartphone, no internet: SMS to 2121, or phone banking on 600 52 5500.
  • Salary card, not an account: the PPC portal — the app cannot see a card-only balance.

Stay safe while you check

FAB never asks for your PIN, OTP or password by phone, SMS, email or WhatsApp. The Central Bank of the UAE directed banks to stop using WhatsApp for customer communication, so FAB does not offer balance checks over WhatsApp — any message offering one is fraudulent. If you suspect fraud, email atyourservice@bankfab.com. Never enter your PIN on a balance-check page — the PPC portal asks for your Card ID, never your PIN.
