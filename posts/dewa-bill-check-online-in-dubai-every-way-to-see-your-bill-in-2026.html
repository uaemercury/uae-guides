DEWA Bill Check Online in Dubai: Every Way to See Your Bill in 2026

Every month, over 3 million Dubai residents open their DEWA bill — the combined electricity and water statement from the Dubai Electricity and Water Authority. The bill is issued monthly and due within 15 days, and checking it takes seconds. There is no single "right" way to do it: the website, the app, WhatsApp, SMS, a phone call and even a kiosk at a Metro station all work. Here is every method, so you can pick the one that fits the moment you are in.

The fastest way: dewa.gov.ae, no login needed

Go to dewa.gov.ae and open the bill enquiry or View Bill service. Enter your 12-digit account number or 9-digit premise number — no login is needed. Your current bill, outstanding balance and due date appear instantly. This is the method to use when you just need the number right now, before paying.

DEWA Smart App and DubaiNow

Download the DEWA Smart App from the App Store or Google Play and log in with your DEWA account or UAE Pass. On the dashboard, tap Bills, then View/Pay Bill to see your current bill, balance and due date. The DubaiNow app offers the same bill view alongside other government services, which is convenient if you already use it for other Dubai services.

WhatsApp with Rammas

Save +971 4 601 9999 in your phone contacts. Open WhatsApp, send "Hi" and choose Bill Enquiry from the Rammas menu. Enter your account number when asked, and Rammas shows your current bill and balance. This is the easiest option if you are already chatting on WhatsApp and do not want to open another app.

SMS and phone call

Text your account number to 4488 and your bill details come back by SMS. Or call the DEWA helpline on 04 601 9999, which runs 24/7 — have your account number and Emirates ID handy, follow the prompts or ask the agent for your current bill and outstanding balance. You can also pay by card over the phone, or note the amount and pay later through the app, website or EasyPay.

Kiosks at Metro stations and malls

DEWA self-service kiosks sit in every Dubai Metro station and major malls, open 24/7. Select Bill Enquiry, then enter your 12-digit account number or 9-digit premise number. Your bill and balance appear on screen — pay by cash (up to AED 10,000) or card and take the receipt. This is the fallback that works when your phone is dead or you have no internet.

Reading the bill, and what to do about a high one

Once you can see the bill, the next step is understanding it: the charges break down into electricity and water consumption, plus municipality and service fees. If a bill suddenly looks high, compare the units consumed with previous months before disputing the dirham total — a spike in units points to a leak or a faulty appliance, while a tariff change shows up differently.
