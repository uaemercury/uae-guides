FAB Balance Check Online: App, Online Banking, SMS and the PPC Portal

You can check your FAB balance online without visiting a branch — but the right method depends on what you hold. Account holders use the FAB Mobile app or FAB Personal Online Banking. Prepaid salary card holders, such as Ratibi card holders, use the PPC portal, because there is no bank account behind the card. Picking the wrong channel is the reason most people think the check "does not work" when it actually works fine somewhere else.

Which online method fits you

If you hold a FAB bank account, use the FAB Mobile app or FAB Online Banking — your balances appear after login. If you hold a prepaid salary card only, use the PPC portal; the app may not show a balance for a card-only product. If you hold both, check the account in the app or online banking and the card on the PPC portal. They are separate, and if you are not sure which type you hold, ask your employer.

FAB was formed in April 2017 when First Gulf Bank and National Bank of Abu Dhabi (NBAD) merged. NBAD no longer exists as a separate bank, and FAB is licensed and regulated by the Central Bank of the UAE. Its official website is bankfab.com — type it yourself, and never follow a balance-check link sent by SMS or email.

Method 1: the FAB Mobile app

The FAB Mobile app is the fastest online method for account holders. It is on the App Store, Google Play and Huawei AppGallery. After login, your account and card balances appear on the home screen, and you can tap an account for recent transactions. The app also manages accounts and cards, shows transactions with logos, categories and smart filters, and sends real-time alerts. You can sign in with fingerprint or face. If the app shows no balance for a card, it may be a card-only prepaid product — use the PPC portal below instead.

Method 2: FAB Online Banking

FAB Personal Online Banking, at bankfab.com, is the best online method on a computer. Log in and open the Accounts view — your balances, recent transactions and history are shown there, and you can view and download statements. It works from anywhere with internet, including abroad, and it is best when you want balances plus statements, not just a single figure. If you cannot sign in, do not keep guessing your password: FAB locks online banking after 3 unsuccessful login attempts. Call 600 52 5500 and the account will be unlocked after verification. From outside the UAE, call +971 2 681 1511.

Method 3: the PPC portal for prepaid and salary cards

If you hold a prepaid salary card, the PPC portal is your online method. No registration or app is needed. The two portals are the FAB portal at ppc.bankfab.com/PPCInquiry/ and the Magnati portal at ppc.magnati.com/ppc-inquiry/ — Magnati is FAB's payments technology partner, and the old PPC NBAD portal is decommissioned.

On the Magnati portal the fields are the last 4 digits of the card number, your Card ID, and a CAPTCHA. Your Card ID is a separate reference number printed on the card or on its welcome-envelope carrier — it is not your card number. The FAB portal asks for the details shown on its own page; enter only what it asks for and submit. Never enter your PIN on a balance-check page.

Method 4: SMS banking

SMS banking works from your registered mobile number: text BAL plus the last 4 digits of your account number to 2121. If it does not work, your number may not be registered for SMS banking — call FAB on 600 52 5500. For a credit card, the keyword is different: CBAL plus the last 4 digits of your card number, also to 2121.

Online or ATM — when to use each

Online methods are best with internet, at home, at work, or abroad. The app is fastest on a phone, online banking is best on a computer, and the PPC portal is the only online method for prepaid salary cards. ATMs are best with no internet, no phone battery, or no smartphone: at a FAB ATM, insert your card, enter your PIN, and select Balance Inquiry. Checks at FAB ATMs are free, and a balance enquiry at another UAE bank's ATM costs AED 1 per transaction under FAB's official schedule of charges.

Staying safe while you check

FAB warns that it never asks for your PIN, OTP or password via phone, SMS, email or WhatsApp. Type bankfab.com yourself, never click a balance-check link in a text or email, and do not enter your PIN, OTP or password on any site other than bankfab.com, the PPC portals above, or the official FAB Mobile app. FAB does not offer balance checks via WhatsApp — in 2026 the Central Bank of the UAE directed all UAE banks to stop using WhatsApp for customer communication and financial services — so WhatsApp balance-check messages are fraudulent. If a message says your account will be blocked unless you click a link, treat it as fraud and report it to FAB on 600 52 5500 or by email to atyourservice@bankfab.com.
