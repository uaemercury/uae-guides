FAB Balance Check in the UAE: Every Way to See Your Balance in 2026

First Abu Dhabi Bank — FAB — is the UAE's largest bank, formed in April 2017 when First Gulf Bank and National Bank of Abu Dhabi merged. If you bank with FAB, checking your balance takes seconds. The trick is picking the right route for what you hold, because FAB runs two separate systems: one for regular bank accounts, and one for prepaid salary cards.

Account or card? It matters

A regular FAB account is a bank account. A Ratibi card is a prepaid salary card issued through your employer under the UAE Wage Protection System. It has no bank account behind it, no chequebook and no overdraft. If your employer handed you a card and you never opened a bank account, you hold a card-only product — and the app and online banking will not show its balance. That is expected, not a fault. The two systems are separate, and using the wrong one is the most common reason people conclude, wrongly, that their money is missing.

The FAB Mobile app

For regular account holders, the FAB Mobile app is the fastest route. It is available on the App Store, Google Play and Huawei AppGallery. After you log in, your account and card balances are listed on the home screen; tap an account to see recent transactions. The app also shows transactions with logos, categories and smart filters, and sends real-time alerts — handy for knowing the moment a salary lands. Biometric login works once you have set it up. If the app shows nothing for a card, check whether it is a Ratibi or prepaid card and use the portal below instead.

Online banking

On a computer, FAB Personal Online Banking is the best option. Type bankfab.com yourself, follow the official link to online banking, log in, and open the Accounts view. You can see balances, full transaction history and downloadable statements — useful when you want to know when money came in or went out. Never reach the login page from a link in an SMS or email. And do not keep guessing your password: FAB locks online banking after three unsuccessful login attempts, and you will need to call customer care to have it unlocked after verification.

At an ATM or by phone

A FAB ATM gives you the balance in under a minute: insert your card, enter your PIN, select Balance Inquiry. You can also print a mini-statement of recent transactions. Checks at FAB ATMs are free; a balance enquiry at another UAE bank's ATM costs AED 1 per transaction under FAB's official schedule of charges.

With no internet at all, phone banking works. Call 600 52 5500 inside the UAE, or +971 2 681 1511 from outside the UAE. Have your account or card details ready and be prepared to confirm your identity. FAB never asks for your PIN, OTP or password by phone, SMS, email or WhatsApp — if someone calls asking for those, hang up and call 600 52 5500 yourself.

SMS and the prepaid card portal

SMS banking works from your registered mobile number: text BAL plus the last four digits of your account number to 2121. For a credit card, the keyword is different — CBAL plus the last four digits of the card number, also to 2121. Never send your full account number, PIN or OTP by text.

Ratibi and other prepaid salary cards are checked on the PPC portal, with no registration or app needed. On the Magnati version, enter the last four digits of the card, plus the Card ID, plus a CAPTCHA. The Card ID is a separate reference number printed on the card or its welcome envelope — it is not the card number. On the FAB PPC version, enter the 16-digit card number plus the CVV.

Which route is fastest for you

Account plus smartphone: the app. Account plus computer: online banking. Ratibi or prepaid card: the PPC portal. No internet: call 600 52 5500. Out and near a cash machine: a FAB ATM. Checking whether salary has landed: salary card holders use the PPC portal, salary account holders look for the salary credit in recent transactions in the app or online banking.
