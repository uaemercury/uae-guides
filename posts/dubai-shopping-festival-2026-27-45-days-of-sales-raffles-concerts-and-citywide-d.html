UAE MERCURY — Your Window to the Emirates

The Dubai Shopping Festival returns for its 32nd edition on 4 December 2026 and runs for 45 days until 17 January 2027 — the longest edition ever. It is the biggest date on the city's retail calendar: discounts of 25 to 75 percent across more than 1,000 brands and 3,500 outlets, raffles with life-changing prizes, concerts, drone shows, fireworks and a month of pop-up experiences across the malls. One warning first, because it catches people every year: searches for the festival can mean last year's 31st edition (5 December 2025 to 11 January 2026) or this year's 32nd edition. Everything in this article is about the 32nd edition, 4 December 2026 to 17 January 2027. For the complete day-by-day guide, see the full dubai shopping festival 2026 breakdown.

How the festival is organised

The festival is run by the Dubai Festivals and Retail Establishment, part of the Department of Economy and Tourism, and it has run every winter since 1996. The 45-day format is new: the 31st edition ran 38 days. The extra week gives shoppers more time, but it also spreads the headline events across the calendar, so the best deals, concerts and raffle draws do not all fall in the same week.

There is no entry ticket for the festival itself. The malls, souks and public shows are free, and only specific concerts and experiences are ticketed. Anyone telling you a DSF ticket costs AED 100 is confusing the festival with last year's Mega Raffle ticket — and that price has not been confirmed for the 32nd edition, so do not budget on it.

The sales: where the real discounts are

The headline numbers are 25 to 75 percent off across the city, with flash sales reaching up to 90 percent. Electronics, gold and jewellery, fashion, perfume, watches and home goods get the deepest cuts, and the Gold Souk typically offers up to 50 percent off selected gold and diamond pieces. The 12-Hour Sale, expected on 26 December from 10am to 10pm at the Majid Al Futtaim malls, is the single best day for discounts of up to 90 percent — but note that it is expected, not yet officially confirmed for this edition, so check before you plan around it.

The smartest way to shop DSF is to follow the signature promotions rather than wandering: Lucky Receipt, Daily Surprises and Shop Scan & Win all reward ordinary purchases with prize entries. Tourists should also remember the VAT refund: you can reclaim 85 percent of VAT on purchases over AED 250, validated at the airport within 90 days.

Raffles: the ENOC draw and the Mega Raffle

Raffles are the festival's other engine. The ENOC DSF raffle is confirmed for this edition, running from 12 October 2026 to 17 January 2027. Entry comes from spending AED 25 at ZOOM, AED 50 at Tasjeel or AED 50 at AutoPro, and the grand prize on 17 January 2027 is AED 500,000 in cash. Weekly draws during the festival award five prizes of AED 100,000 plus a gold bar.

The Mega Raffle is the famous one — last year it gave away a Nissan every day plus AED 100,000, with a grand prize of AED 400,000 on the final day and more than AED 11 million in prizes overall. But the 32nd-edition ticket price and prize list have not been confirmed, so do not assume the AED 100 ticket from last year. The gold raffle, run by the Dubai Gold & Jewellery Group, returns with 25 kilograms of gold and diamond prizes.

Concerts, fireworks and drone shows

The festival promises more than 40 concerts, but the full lineup had not been announced as of early November 2026. What is confirmed is that the e& DSF Nights return to Dubai Festival City Mall at Festival Bay, with local artists and weekend concerts ticketed through Platinumlist, and that Saint Levant appears in the official 32nd-edition promo video at MOTB. The fireworks and drone show schedule for this edition is also not yet confirmed; last year's pattern was a nightly drone show at 8pm and 10pm at Bluewaters and The Beach JBR, fireworks on Friday and Saturday evenings, and a nightly fireworks and IMAGINE show at Dubai Festival City Mall.

Two new chapters are confirmed for this edition. The etisalat Beach Canteen runs at Sunset Beach from 10 to 27 December, the first time it has been part of the festival. The etisalat MOTB, the region's biggest street-food and fashion market, runs at Dubai Design District from 8 to 24 January 2027. The DSF Auto Season runs through the closing days, and the DSF x Hatta Wadi Hub offers free family activities, outdoor cinema and food pop-ups through December.

Planning your visit

The festival is at its best in the first two weeks, when the opening energy is highest and the malls are still manageable, and again in the final week, when retailers clear stock. Mid-festival weekdays are quieter than weekends, and evenings after 7pm are when the city comes alive. December weather helps: average highs around 26°C and lows around 16°C, so outdoor evenings are comfortable.

If you are combining the festival with other seasonal attractions, the city's big winter show runs alongside it — the Global Village Season 31 season runs from 14 October 2026 to 17 May 2027, with entry at AED 25 on weekdays and AED 30 for any-day tickets. Many visitors pair a DSF shopping day with an evening at Global Village, and the two events share the same December-to-January peak.

The practical advice is simple: decide what you actually want to buy before you go, follow the raffle mechanics for anything you would buy anyway, and keep an eye on the official programme as December approaches, because the concert lineup and the Mega Raffle details are still to come. The festival rewards the organised shopper far more than the impulse buyer.
