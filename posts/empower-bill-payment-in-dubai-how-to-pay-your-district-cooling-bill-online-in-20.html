Empower Bill Payment in Dubai — How to Pay Your District Cooling Bill Online in 2026

Your Empower bill is not your DEWA bill. Empower is Dubai's district cooling provider, and its bill is separate — you cannot pay it on the DEWA website. The good news is that paying it online takes just a few minutes, and you do not even need to sign in.

What is Empower, and what is district cooling?

District cooling is the central production and supply of chilled water for air conditioning. Instead of each building running its own chillers, the air conditioning is provided by an off-site plant and sent to your home or office through chilled water pipes.

Empower is the world's largest district cooling services provider.

Because Empower is a separate entity from DEWA, your DEWA bill and your Empower bill are two different bills. The 9-digit premise number on a DEWA bill is not your Empower account number — your Empower account number is 10 digits long.

How to pay your Empower bill online

The quickest route is the official e-services portal at e-services.empower.ae. Open the portal and choose "Pay Now" without signing in, or log in. Enter your 10-digit Empower account number. Check the bill amount and the due date. Then pay by credit card or net banking.

You do not need to sign in or register for a one-off payment. The payment section of the portal offers guest payment, and login is only needed to manage your account — for example, to check your payment history or set up Auto Pay.

The amount payable on the portal is the same as on your bill.

If you sign in, you can also manage your account online: check your payment history and set up Auto Pay (go to "My Services" and follow the screens). Only use the official portal — e-services.empower.ae — before entering your card details. If you are unsure, call Empower on 800 3276.

Other ways to pay your Empower bill

If you prefer not to pay online, there are five other ways to pay an Empower bill.

At a bank: deposit the payable amount at a branch of HSBC Bank Middle East, Mashreq Bank or Emirates NBD, then email the deposit slip or remittance advice, with your invoice number and your 10-digit account number, to creditcontrol@empower.ae.

At an ATM: pay cash at Emirates NBD's ATMs or cash/cheque deposit machines — choose the utility bill payment option. In cash: pay at Al Ansari Exchange branches across the UAE — give the teller your 10-digit account number.

On DubaiNow: use the DubaiNow app to pay your Empower bill through mPay (register at mpay.dubai.ae). In person: pay by cash, cheque or credit card at Empower's customer service counters and at the Empower head office in Al Jadaf, Dubai.

New customer? Register first

If you are a new customer, you must register with Empower before you can pay your bill online. Registration is free for new customers, and the connection fee (also known as the activation charge) varies according to the project and building.

In the Empower e-services portal, open "New Registration". Enter your 9-digit DEWA premise number, choose "Owner" or "Tenant" and continue. Enter your property and personal details, then submit.

Attach the required documents — the tenancy contract (Ejari) for tenants, the title deed or sale/assignment agreement for owners, your Emirates ID and your passport. After processing, you will receive your Empower account number by SMS or email. The confirmation message will show the security deposit and the connection fee.

Pay the required amount online with a debit/credit card, or pay in cash, debit/credit card or cheque at an Empower office or service centre.

Your service will be connected within 1 to 3 working days after the payment is received and any due amount is cleared. Once your account is activated, you can pay your bills through the e-services portal.

What your bill is made of

A district cooling bill is normally made up of a demand (capacity) charge, a consumption charge and a fuel surcharge. The demand charge (also called the capacity charge) is AED 750 per refrigeration ton (RT) per year, and is billed monthly in advance.

The consumption charge is based on AED 0.568 per RT per hour. The fuel surcharge is set under the guidance of the Supreme Council of Energy.

Meter maintenance charges (AED 50 per quarter for buildings with live meters, or AED 30 per month when meters are not installed) and connection fees may also be charged.

The exact rates for your building depend on your contract, so the figure on your bill is the one that applies. If a charge looks wrong, check the meter reading and the billed period first, then call Empower on 800 3276.

Stay on the official portal

The Empower e-services portal is separate from the Empower website, so always check that you are on the official e-services.empower.ae domain. Never enter your card details on a lookalike page.
