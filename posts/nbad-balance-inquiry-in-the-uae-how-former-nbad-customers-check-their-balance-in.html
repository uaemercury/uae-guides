UAE MERCURY — Your Window to the Emirates

If you are searching for an NBAD balance inquiry, here is the short answer: NBAD no longer exists.

Your account is now a First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB) account, and you check it with FAB's methods. There is no NBAD app, no NBAD online banking portal, and no NBAD balance service any more.

What happened to NBAD

The National Bank of Abu Dhabi (NBAD) merged with First Gulf Bank in April 2017 to form First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB). NBAD no longer exists as a separate bank. FAB is licensed and regulated by the Central Bank of the UAE, and its official website is bankfab.com.

Former NBAD account holders became FAB account holders at the merger. Account numbers and cards transferred automatically.

You did not need to open a new account, and you do not need to open one now. If you held an NBAD account, it is a FAB account today, under the same account number unless FAB told you otherwise.

If you are unsure about your account, call FAB on 600 52 5500 and ask them to confirm it. Have your account or card details ready, and never read out your PIN or OTP on a call you did not make yourself.

The old NBAD portals are dead

There is no separate NBAD online banking portal any more. Old NBAD portal URLs redirect to bankfab.com or are dead. Do not search for an NBAD login page, and do not enter your details on any site that claims to be NBAD online banking.

The only official FAB website is bankfab.com. Type it yourself. Never follow a link from a text message or an email. Any page claiming to be NBAD online banking is either outdated or a scam.

The same applies to prepaid cards. The old PPC NBAD portal is decommissioned. The active prepaid portals are the FAB portal and the Magnati portal.

Old "Ratibi NBAD" cards carried over into FAB and Magnati after the 2017 merger. Check them on the current portals, not on any old NBAD address.

How to check your balance now: the FAB Mobile app

The easiest method for former NBAD account holders is the FAB Mobile app. It is available on the App Store, Google Play and Huawei AppGallery. After login, your account balance appears on the home screen, with transactions listed below.

The app also lets you manage accounts and debit and credit cards, view transactions with logos, categories and smart filters, and receive real-time alerts. You can sign up for a product with just an Emirates ID.

If you never set up the FAB app, download it and register with your FAB account details. Your old NBAD login does not work. Use your current FAB credentials. If you have forgotten them, use the app's recovery options or call 600 52 5500.

Online banking and ATM

FAB Online Banking at bankfab.com is the second main method. Log in through the official site, go to Accounts, and you will see your balance, statements and transaction history. This is the best option if you want a full statement rather than a quick balance.

You can also check at a FAB ATM. Insert your card, enter your PIN, and select Balance Inquiry or a mini-statement.

FAB ATMs are free for this check. A balance enquiry at another UAE bank's ATM costs AED 1 per transaction under FAB's official schedule of charges. Cover the keypad when you type your PIN.

Phone banking

Phone banking is the fourth method. Call FAB customer care on 600 52 5500 in the UAE, or +971 2 681 1511 from outside the UAE. These numbers are verified live on bankfab.com.

Phone banking is most useful when you are abroad and cannot use the app or online banking. Have your account details ready before you call, and never share your PIN or OTP with anyone, including anyone claiming to be from the bank.

Old NBAD cards: what still works

Old NBAD debit and credit cards were replaced by FAB cards after the merger. If you still hold an old NBAD card, check whether it is still valid. A card with an expiry date in the past will not work, and you will need a replacement FAB card.

Contact FAB on 600 52 5500 to ask for a replacement. Do not keep using an expired card, and do not enter its details on any balance-check website. For prepaid salary cards, use the PPC portals: ppc.bankfab.com/PPCInquiry/ and ppc.magnati.com/ppc-inquiry/ — typed into your browser yourself.

What FAB never asks for

FAB warns that it never asks for PIN, OTP or password via phone, SMS, email or WhatsApp. If any balance page asks for your PIN or OTP, close it at once. Never enter your PIN, OTP or CVV on any balance page you reached through a link in a message.
