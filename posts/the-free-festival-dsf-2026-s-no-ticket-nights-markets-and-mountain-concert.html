UAE MERCURY — Your Window to the Emirates

Most visitors hear "shopping festival" and picture a mall. The surprise of the Dubai Shopping Festival is how much of it happens outdoors, in public, for free. The 32nd edition runs from Friday 4 December 2026 to Sunday 17 January 2027 — 45 days, the longest ever — and while the sales fill the malls by day, the evenings belong to free shows, free markets and free concerts spread across the waterfront, the beach and even the mountains at Hatta. This is a guide to that free festival, the one running alongside the sales.

The nightly show that costs nothing

Every night from Saturday 5 December 2026 to Sunday 17 January 2027, the waterfront between The Beach at JBR and Bluewaters Island hosts the DSF Light Show from 7:30pm to 10:30pm. It is this edition's headline spectacle: architectural laser mapping across the buildings, two drone shows set to music each night, and a music-commanded laser show. There is no ticket, no booking and no bad seat — you watch from the JBR beachfront or from the Bluewaters boardwalk across the water.

A note of caution for planners: no fireworks displays have been announced for the 32nd edition yet. Previous seasons paired drone shows at this waterfront with fireworks on some nights, and an announcement may still come, but the Light Show is the confirmed nightly event. Check the official programme on mydsf.ae closer to December for any additions.

Free days out, December and January

The free programme starts before the festival itself. On Wednesday 2 December 2026, DSF x Hatta Wadi Hub hosts a free candlelight concert of Khaleeji music for Eid Al Etihad — a mountain evening that could hardly be further from a shopping mall. Then, from 10 to 27 December, the etisalat Beach Canteen takes over Sunset Beach in Jumeirah with food pop-ups, live entertainment and workshops. Entry is free; you pay only for what you eat and buy.

January's free highlight is etisalat MOTB — Market Outside The Box — at Dubai Design District (d3) from 8 to 24 January 2027. Now in its 14th season, it is an open-air market of independent brands, food concepts, immersive installations and live entertainment, again with free entry. It is the festival's closing chapter, and it keeps going for a week after the festival's official end on 17 January. Add to all this the free mall activations and roaming shows that run all season, several free weekday DSF Nights shows at Dubai Festival City Mall, and the simple pleasure of wandering the Deira Gold Souk and Spice Souk, and a visitor could fill a week without paying an entry fee anywhere.

What is ticketed — and what the sales look like

Only specific events charge. The opening concerts at Coca-Cola Arena are the main ones: Rashid Al Majed on Friday 4 December, with tickets from AED 395 via Platinumlist, and Steve Aoki on Saturday 5 December. Some DSF Nights weekend shows at Festival City are also ticketed. Everything else in this article is free to walk into.

The shopping, of course, is the other half of the festival. Retailer offers for this edition had not been announced as of 17 November 2026, but the expected pattern is a citywide sale season from 26 December at 25–75% off — last season covered more than 1,000 brands — with the deepest cuts late in the season and a one-day 12-Hour Sale expected on 26 December with up to 90% off at Majid Al Futtaim malls. DSF Auto Season also runs citywide from 19 December to 17 January for anyone whose idea of window-shopping involves cars.

Keep the whole season in view

Festival dates shift, offers publish in late November, and free events get added as the programme fills in — which is exactly why a single events hub helps. For DSF dates alongside the rest of Dubai's winter season, bookmark UAE Mercury's Dubai events hub and check back as December approaches.

Come for the sales if you like. Stay for the evenings — in Dubai this winter, the best seat in the city is a free one on the waterfront.
