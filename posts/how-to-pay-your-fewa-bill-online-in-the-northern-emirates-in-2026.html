How to Pay Your FEWA Bill Online in the Northern Emirates in 2026

If you live in Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah or Fujairah, your electricity and water come from Etihad Water and Electricity (EtihadWE), formerly FEWA.

You can pay your monthly bill online in about three minutes without logging in. This guide covers every official method, fastest first — and the limits you need to know before you pay.

The fastest way: View and Pay, no login

The fastest way to pay is the View and Pay Bill service on the EtihadWE website — the no-login flow most guides call FEWA Quick Pay. The official page states that customers can pay without logging into their account through this option.

Open the EtihadWE website at etihadwe.ae. From the main menu, go to Services and select View and Pay. Enter your account number — the number printed on your bill — and double-check it, because the payment goes to the account you enter.

Then choose your payment option, agree to the Terms and Conditions, and enter your card details. The process takes about three minutes, 24 hours a day. Keep the confirmation or receipt until the amount shows as paid.

You need three things: your FEWA account number, a valid email address, and a GCC-issued credit card.

The limits to know before you pay

The maximum payment by credit card is AED 10,000. If your bill is higher, split it across days or use another channel. Some guides quote higher limits — the official page states AED 10,000.

A fee of 0.0068 applies to amounts above AED 10,000. That is the figure exactly as the official page states it, with no unit or percentage sign — so check the final amount on screen before confirming.

Only GCC-issued credit cards are accepted for online card payments, and a credit card can be used at most 5 times per day. The payment is confirmed on screen in about three minutes, but allow one working day for it to be fully reflected.

The service code is 357-04-002-000. It is useful if you ever need to reference the payment service with EtihadWE.

Want bill history and saved cards? Use the e-services portal

For bill history, saved cards or several accounts, use the e-services portal. Open the EtihadWE website, click Login, select Customer, and log in using UAE PASS. Then pick the account, click Pay Now, and use a saved card or enter a new one.

Logging in pays off if you pay monthly — accounts stay saved and past bills are in one place. No UAE PASS yet? Set it up first: it is also the login for the DEWA and DubaiNow apps.

Pay in the EtihadWE mobile app

The EtihadWE app is on Google Play and the Apple App Store. Open the app, log in using UAE PASS or proceed through the available payment option, select the account, choose your payment option, and complete the payment.

Like the website it works 24 hours a day, takes about three minutes, and keeps your accounts saved after the first login.

Pay through your bank

Banks are an official channel with the same terms: 24 hours a day, about three minutes to submit. Pay through your own bank's online or mobile banking bill-payment service, using your FEWA account number as the reference.

If the bill is due today, use the website or app instead. Official channels confirm immediately, while bank processing can differ.

Paying in person: kiosks, exchange centres, service centres

Self-service kiosks run around the clock and take about three minutes. Find them in Lulu Hypermarkets, EPPCO and ADNOC petrol stations and major malls. Choose the FEWA bill payment option, enter your account number, and pay by card or cash.

You can also pay at Al Ansari Exchange branches — open Saturday to Thursday, about 10 minutes. Al Ansari is the only exchange house named in the official EtihadWE channel list.

Or visit a customer happiness centre during official working hours — about 10 minutes per transaction — in Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Al Dhaid, Fujairah, Dibba Al Fujairah or Ras Al Khaimah.

Go here if your case needs a person: a disputed bill, a payment that did not reflect, or a new connection.

Emirates Post is listed by several third-party guides as another in-person option, but it does not appear in the channel list on the official EtihadWE Bill Payment page. Confirm at your nearest post office before relying on it.

If something goes wrong

Wrong account number? Check before you confirm: payments go to the account number you typed, and recovering a wrong payment is slow.

Payment made but the bill still shows unpaid? Allow one working day, the official expected time. If it still shows as unpaid, contact EtihadWE with the receipt and account number.

Card declined? The usual causes are a card that is not GCC-issued, an amount over AED 10,000, or five uses of the card that day. Try another GCC card, a kiosk or an exchange centre.

If the bill amount looks wrong, do not pay it. Check the bill's itemised lines first, then raise it with EtihadWE.
