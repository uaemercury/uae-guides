UAE MERCURY — Your Window to the Emirates

First, the trap: not every Dubai Christmas market that looks free is free, and not every date doing the rounds is confirmed. One popular market has not announced its 2026 dates at all, one has moved across the city from its old home, and the season's biggest new festival had not published its ticket prices as of mid-November 2026. None of that makes the season weak — it makes it one where the details matter. Here is the version with the confirmed, the expected and the unconfirmed kept clearly apart.

What is confirmed, and when it opens

The season opens on 1 December 2026 with the brand-new DXB Winter Festival on the waterfront beside Dubai Festival City Mall, running until 10 January 2027 across a 20,000-square-metre site with more than 150 vendors, a European-style market, Santa's Grotto, a funfair and fireworks over Dubai Creek. It is ticketed — standard prices were still to be announced as of mid-November — but the first 10,000 tickets carry a 25% early-bird discount for those who register, and one ticket covers everything inside. The Madinat Jumeirah Festive Market follows on 4 December and runs to 27 December on Fort Island: free entry, no booking, opening at 3pm on weekdays and noon at weekends, built around a 36-foot tree, wooden stalls, a Venetian carousel, a North Pole train, a snow fight zone and abra rides with Santa. Winter City at Expo City Dubai runs 11–31 December, daily 4pm to 10pm, from AED 60 per person. Winter District runs 12–28 December at Jumeirah Emirates Towers and is free to enter.

What has moved, and what is still unconfirmed

Winter District is the mover: in previous years it was held at Dubai Media City Amphitheatre, but for 2026 its home is the plaza at Jumeirah Emirates Towers, with the Museum of the Future behind it. Some listings show 13 December as its start date rather than 12 December, so treat the opening day as expected and confirm before travelling; its 2pm-to-midnight hours, running to 1am at weekends, are also based on the 2025 edition. The Winter Garden at Al Habtoor Palace is the unconfirmed one: a long-standing favourite, expected to return to Al Habtoor City in early December, but with no 2026 dates announced as of 17 November 2026. Its expected AED 25 entry, fully redeemable inside, is the 2025 price. Plan around it by all means — the canal-side market, food kiosks and play area are worth it — but check the venue's channels first.

What each market actually costs

Two of the big markets are completely free to enter: Madinat Jumeirah and Winter District — you pay only for food, drinks, rides and shopping inside. The Ripe Festive Market at Dubai Police Academy Park charges a token AED 5 for adults on December weekends, with children under 12 free; it is also the only market on the list that runs in daylight, from 9am on Saturdays and Sundays, with wreath-making, Santa visits and carols alongside its usual farmers and food trucks. Winter City costs from AED 60, or AED 175 for the Express Entry Pass, with under-3s, People of Determination and one caregiver free. Global Village — not a Christmas market, but the season's biggest evening out, open since 14 October 2026 until May 2027 — costs AED 25 on weekdays and AED 30 for an any-day ticket. Even at the free markets, a family of four should budget AED 100–200 for food and activities inside.

The dates that catch people out

Christmas Day 2026 falls on Friday 25 December, and Madinat Jumeirah, the DXB Winter Festival, Winter City and Winter District are all open on the day itself. The catch comes afterwards: Madinat Jumeirah closes on 27 December, so it is not open between Christmas and New Year, and Winter City ends on 31 December. Only the DXB Winter Festival stays open through New Year's Day — it is also the only major festive site open on New Year's Eve. Most markets open in the afternoon and run late, with the busiest hours between 7pm and 10pm on Fridays and Saturdays; arriving at opening time is the reliable way to get photos and short queues. Tree hunters should note that Dubai has no single public Christmas tree: the biggest is Madinat Jumeirah's 36-foot tree, with others at the Festival City waterfront, under the Al Wasl Dome, and in the atriums of Dubai Festival City Mall, Mall of the Emirates and Dubai Mall from late November.

Plan it properly

Match the market to the trip: Madinat Jumeirah for atmosphere, Winter City for young children, the DXB Winter Festival for scale and New Year, Winter District for a free Metro-friendly evening, and the Ripe Market for a festive morning. For the wider season — cloud-hosted dates, timings and planning notes gathered in one place — the UAE Mercury events hub is a useful starting point before December begins.

Market dates and programmes can shift as December approaches, so confirm on each venue's official channels before setting out — especially for the two listings still marked expected or unconfirmed.
