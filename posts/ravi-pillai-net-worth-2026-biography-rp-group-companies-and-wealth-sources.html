Ravi Pillai Net Worth 2026: Biography, RP Group Companies, and Wealth Sources

This is a fact-sheet-style profile of B. Ravi Pillai, the Dubai-based chairman of the RP Group, compiled from Forbes list data and secondary reporting. For the full narrative version of his story, see the detailed breakdown of the Ravi Pillai fortune.

Quick facts

  • Full name: Balachandra Ravi Pillai (B. Ravi Pillai)
  • Born: 2 September 1953, Chavara village, Kollam district, Kerala, India
  • Background: Farming family; first ventures were a chit fund company and a small Kerala construction firm
  • Residence: Dubai, United Arab Emirates
  • Position: Chairman and Managing Director, RP Group of Companies
  • Known as: "Ambani of the Gulf"

Biography: the key dates

1953 — Born in Chavara, Kerala, into a farming family.

Early 1970s — Runs a chit fund company in Kerala; later starts a small construction business.

Mid-1970s — The Kerala construction business collapses after a labour strike. The failure teaches him the operational lesson that shapes his career: in contracting, labour management and delivery discipline are everything.

1978 — Migrates to Saudi Arabia at 25 during the Kingdom's oil-fuelled construction boom. With a well-connected Saudi partner, founds the Nasser S. Al Hajri Corporation (NSH) with 150 employees.

1980s–1990s — NSH grows into one of the largest general contractors in the Middle East. Profits are reinvested into capacity and then into adjacent sectors.

2000s–2010s — The RP Group takes shape as a diversified conglomerate headquartered in Dubai: construction, steel, cement, hospitality (Raviz Resorts, Leela Kovalam stake), healthcare (Upasana Hospital), oil and gas, IT, real estate, trading, travel, tourism, education.

2008 — Receives the Pravasi Bharatiya Samman from the Government of India.

2010 — Receives the Padma Shri, India's fourth-highest civilian honour.

2014–2015 — Featured among Forbes International's India rich lists; ranked by Arabian Business as the fourth most powerful Indian in the Middle East.

2023 — Reported as the first Indian to purchase a Rs 100 crore Airbus helicopter.

December 2024 — Receives Bahrain's Medal of Efficiency from King Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa in the royal palace.

2026 — Forbes values him at $4.1 billion (India list, #71) and $4.2 billion (world list).

RP Group: companies and sectors

The group's structure, as described in secondary reporting:

  • Construction & contracting — Nasser S. Al Hajri Corporation (flagship); among the Middle East's largest general contractors
  • Steel & cement — industrial operations supplying the group's own projects
  • Hospitality — Raviz Resorts (five-star chain across Kerala); stake in Leela Kovalam
  • Healthcare — Upasana Hospital and related ventures
  • Real estate — portfolio across the Gulf and India, including Burj Khalifa apartments
  • Energy & services — oil and gas services, IT, trading
  • Consumer & travel — travel, tourism, education

Scale indicators (secondary reports): turnover in the multi-billion-dollar range; 70,000–120,000+ employees across nine countries; consistently cited among the largest employers of Indians in the Middle East and a major remitter of foreign currency to India.

Wealth sources, ranked

  1. Contracting profits, compounded over 45+ years — the foundation; every other asset was bought with construction cash flows.
  2. Industrial assets — steel and cement operations with captive demand from group projects.
  3. Hospitality assets — Kerala coastal land acquired cheap, developed into luxury resorts during the tourism boom.
  4. Real estate — Gulf and Indian property accumulated over decades.
  5. Investment stakes — banks, hotels, and real estate in India.

Personal notes

Pillai is known as a deeply religious Hindu; his annual birthday pooja (udayasthamana) at the Tirupati temple is a long-standing tradition. He is regarded as one of the wealthiest Hindu businessmen in Dubai. His philanthropy — temple contributions, educational institutions, Kerala development projects — is substantial but low-profile.

How he compares

Among Gulf-based Indian billionaires, Pillai sits in the top tier but below the very largest fortunes. On the Forbes India 2026 list he ranked 71st at $4.1 billion — behind fellow Kerala-origin billionaire M.A. Yusuff Ali ($5.75 billion, #47) but firmly in the country's billionaire elite. His distinction is the self-made path: no inheritance, one failed business, and a 150-worker startup in a foreign country at 25.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ravi Pillai richer than M.A. Yusuff Ali? No — on the Forbes India 2026 list, Yusuff Ali ($5.75 billion, #47) ranked well above Pillai ($4.1 billion, #71). Both are Kerala-origin billionaires based in the Gulf, but their fortunes come from entirely different industries: retail for Yusuff Ali, construction and industrials for Pillai.

Where does Ravi Pillai live? He is based in Dubai, UAE, where the RP Group is headquartered, though the group's operations span Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Qatar, and other Gulf states plus India.

What does RP Group stand for? The group grew out of the Nasser S. Al Hajri Corporation, founded in Saudi Arabia in 1978; "RP" reflects the founder's name, Ravi Pillai, and the group is formally known as the RP Group of Companies.

How did Ravi Pillai start? After a chit fund venture and a failed construction business in Kerala, he moved to Saudi Arabia in 1978 and co-founded a contracting firm with 150 workers. That firm became one of the Middle East's largest contractors, and its profits funded the diversified RP Group.
