A FAB balance check for salary means checking the balance of the account your salary is paid into.

This page is for salaried account holders — people whose employer pays their salary into a FAB bank account. If your salary goes onto a prepaid salary card instead, such as Ratibi, the check is different.

Salary account or salary card: which do you have

This is the first question to answer, because the two are checked in different places. A salary account is a FAB bank account. Your employer transfers your salary into it each pay cycle. You hold an account number, and usually a debit card linked to that account.

A salary card is a prepaid card, such as Ratibi. There is no bank account behind it. Your employer loads your salary onto the card, and you can only spend what has been loaded.

Ratibi is built for workers earning up to AED 5,000 per month, and it has no chequebook and no overdraft.

The FAB Mobile app may not show a balance for a card-only product. Salary cards are checked on the PPC portal, not in the app. If you are unsure which you have, ask your employer.

Checking a salary account in the FAB Mobile app

The FAB Mobile app is the fastest way to check a salary account. It is available on the App Store, Google Play and Huawei AppGallery. After login, your account balance appears on the home screen, with transactions listed below.

You can also manage accounts and debit and credit cards, view transactions with logos, categories and smart filters, and receive real-time alerts. To check your salary, open the app and look at the account your salary is paid into.

Check the transaction list, not just the balance. The salary credit should appear as a separate entry, with its amount and date. If the amount looks wrong, the transaction list tells you exactly what landed and when.

Checking in online banking

FAB Online Banking at bankfab.com shows the same information as the app, with more room for statements. Log in through the official site — type bankfab.com yourself, never follow a link from a text message or email — then go to Accounts.

You will see your balance, your statements and your full transaction history. Online banking is best if you want to download a statement or check several months of salary credits. The app is better for a quick daily check. Both show the same balance.

Salary credit alerts

The FAB Mobile app offers real-time alerts. Set up an alert for incoming credits, and your phone will tell you the moment your salary lands. This is the simplest salary check of all: you do not need to open the app and look. The alert does the work.

Alerts also help you spot problems early. If no alert comes on your usual pay day, check with your employer. If a transaction you do not recognise appears, call FAB on 600 52 5500 straight away.

Checking a salary card: the PPC portal

If your salary goes onto a Ratibi or another prepaid salary card, do not use the app. Use the Prepaid Card Inquiry (PPC) portal. There are two official portals: the FAB portal at ppc.bankfab.com/PPCInquiry/ and the Magnati portal at ppc.magnati.com/ppc-inquiry/.

Magnati is FAB's payments technology partner. No registration or app is needed. Type the address into your browser yourself. On the Magnati portal the fields are the last 4 digits of the card, your Card ID, and a CAPTCHA — verified on the live portal.

The Card ID is a separate reference number printed on the card or on its welcome-envelope carrier. It is NOT the card number. The portal never asks for your PIN or OTP.

Two other ways to check a salary card

You can also check a salary card at a FAB ATM, or in the Payit wallet app. At an ATM, insert the card, enter your PIN, and select Balance Inquiry. FAB ATMs are free for this check. A balance enquiry at another UAE bank's ATM costs AED 1 per transaction.

In Payit, register as a "Ratibi Cardholder": download Payit, select "Ratibi Cardholder", scan your Emirates ID, confirm your details, then add your card ID and the last 2 digits of your card number.

Enter your mobile number, enter the 6-digit SMS code, and create a 4-digit PIN — then check the balance in the app.

If your salary has not landed

First, check the transaction list, not just the balance. The salary may have landed under a different date than you expected. Second, check with your employer — the portal and the app show money that has actually arrived, not money promised but not sent.

If your employer confirms the salary was sent and nothing shows, call FAB on 600 52 5500. Never share your login password or OTP with anyone. FAB warns that it never asks for PIN, OTP or password via phone, SMS, email or WhatsApp.
